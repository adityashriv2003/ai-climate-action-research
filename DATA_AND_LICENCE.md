NESO describes `actual` as an estimate based on metered generation. The study uses it as a national average operational carbon-intensity estimate, not direct stack measurement, marginal emissions, or lifecycle CO2-equivalent intensity. The archived historical `forecast` column is excluded because the CSV does not supply a forecast-issue timestamp needed to authenticate a fixed as-issued horizon.
